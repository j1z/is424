<h1>The Brown Bear</h1>
<p>The brown bear (Ursus arctos) is one of the largest and most widely distributed land-based carnivorans in the world. Renowned for its immense strength and prominent shoulder hump, this majestic mammal inhabits the dense forests and mountains of North America, Europe, and Asia.</p>
